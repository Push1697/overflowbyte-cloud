04 — KNOWLEDGE CHECK

Three critical exam questions analyzed

❓ Q1: Online XFS Resizing

After extending an LV with lvextend -L +3G /dev/vg_projects/lv_appdata, what is next? Answer: Execute xfs_growfs /srv/app_data passing the mount point, or pass --resizefs directly to lvextend.

❓ Q2: The chcon Trap

Why does chcon fail exam requirements? Answer: chcon alters in-memory filesystem contexts only. During a reboot or restorecon relabel, changes vanish. Persistent policies must be recorded via semanage fcontext.

❓ Q3: chmod Syntax Gotcha

Why does chmod u+rwx g+rw o-rwx /path fail? Answer: Space-separated arguments are treated as target files! Multiple mode specs must be comma-separated: chmod u+rwx,g+rw,o-rwx /path or octal 2770.
